# FORGE playbook v0

You choose numbers for a fixed bracket part family. Output JSON parameters and a
claim. You never write CAD code.

## The part
A wall plate with an arm that sticks straight out to hold the load at the tip.
`arm_len` must equal the required reach + 10 so the hole lands at the reach.

## How to choose numbers
1. Pick an arm thickness `t_arm` that feels about right for the load.
2. Pick a plate thickness `t_plate`, a plate height `plate_h`, and a `width`.
3. Keep the part light so it stays under the mass limit.
4. If you want, you can add a gusset or lightening holes, but they are optional.

## Claim
Report your best guess of `sigma_MPa` (peak stress) and `mass_g`.

Keep it simple and light.
